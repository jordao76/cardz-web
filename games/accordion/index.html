Squeeze the deck into one pile

Accordion

One deck Extra game · download

Goal: squeeze the whole deck into one pile.

  • Tap the stock to deal its next card face up onto the end of the row. Deal whenever you like.
  • A pile may move onto the pile directly to its left, or three places to its left, when their top cards match in rank or suit. The whole pile moves, never part of it.
  • The row closes up behind a pile that moves.
  • You win when the stock is spent and a single pile is left.
  • Your score is the number of piles left, counting each card still in the stock as one; lower is better, 1 for a win.
Read its design file 37 lines
