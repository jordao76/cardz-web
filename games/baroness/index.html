Pairs to thirteen, Kings alone

Baroness

One deck Extra game · download

Goal: discard all 52 cards, in pairs that total 13 and Kings alone.

  • Five cards are dealt face up to start five piles. Only the top card of each pile is available.
  • Drag an available card onto its partner, the top card of another pile, and both go to the discard: Ace + Queen, 2 + Jack, 3 + 10, 4 + 9, 5 + 8, or 6 + 7.
  • A King is worth 13 on its own: drag it to the discard.
  • Tap the stock to deal five more cards, one onto each pile, filling any empty pile. Discard what you can first: a new row covers the tops.
  • The last two cards are the grace cards: they are dealt face up side by side at the end of the row, and are available like the piles' tops.
  • The game is lost when no discard is left and the stock is spent. Your score is the number of cards discarded, 52 for a win.
Read its design file 46 lines

Fourteen Out's pairing at thirteen, with Pyramid's King that leaves alone, over five piles that a stock tap deals a row onto. Two deal steps with conditions share the tap, so the last two cards go to grace cards of their own. This is the file the download button gives you. Make a game explains every part of it.
