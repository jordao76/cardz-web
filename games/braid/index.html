Twenty cards braided down the middle

Braid

Two decks Extra game · download
Also known as
Der Zopf, Plait, Pigtail, Herringbone

Goal: build both decks onto eight foundations, up in suit from a base rank the deal sets, turning the corner from King to Ace.

  • The first card turned from the stock starts a foundation, and its rank is the base of all eight: with an 8, every foundation starts on an 8 and ends on a 7. Each card of that rank starts an empty foundation.
  • The braid of twenty cards runs down the middle, never two of the same card. Only its end card is available, and only to a foundation.
  • The four corners play to the foundations. A space there is refilled at once from the braid's end.
  • The eight sides play to the foundations. A space there takes the top waste card, whenever you choose.
  • Turn the stock one card at a time to the waste; the top waste card is available. You may redeal twice (three passes in all).
  • The braid must be used up to win. Your score is the number of cards on the foundations, 104 for a win.
Read its design file 99 lines

A base read from the deal, as Canfield's, for eight foundations of two decks, capped at thirteen cards so a finished one cannot take its base's twin. A braid of twenty single cards placed in a zig-zag, each uncovered by the next and dealt by an each step that keeps twins out, whose end refills four corners by themselves, and eight sideways cells that take only the waste, when the player chooses.
