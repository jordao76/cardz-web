Climb row by row to the Privy Council

British Constitution

88 cards
Also known as
Constitution

Goal: build eight foundations up from Ace to Jack, each in a single suit. The game uses two decks without their Kings and Queens.

  • Below the Aces lie four rows of eight cards. Only the top row, the Privy Council, plays to the foundations.
  • The Privy Council builds down in alternating colour (e.g. a red 6 on a black 7), one card at a time, from its own row or from the row just below.
  • A gap in any of the top three rows is filled with any card from the row just below. You choose which one, so cards climb a row at a time.
  • A card may jump rows: it goes straight up to a farther row, or onto a foundation, when every row it passes has a gap, just as if it had climbed through them one at a time.
  • A gap in the bottom row is filled from the stock at once. Stock cards never play straight to the foundations.
  • You lose when nothing more can be played.
  • Your score is the number of cards on the foundations, 88 for a win.
