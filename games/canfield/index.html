Foundations can start anywhere

Canfield

One deck

Goal: build all four foundations up in suit from the dealt base rank, wrapping past King back to Ace.

  • The first foundation card is dealt — its rank sets the base for all four foundations. Build each one up in the same suit, wrapping King→Ace as needed.
  • Tableau columns build down in alternating colour and also wrap (a King may go on an Ace). Move a single card or a valid run.
  • The reserve of 13 cards feeds its top card into play; uncovering the next card turns it up.
  • Draw three at a time from the stock to the waste; recycle the waste as often as you like.
  • Empty a column and the reserve refills it at once. Once the reserve is spent, an empty column is yours to fill with any card or run.
