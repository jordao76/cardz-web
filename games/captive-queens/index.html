Fives down, Sixes up, round a court of Queens

Captive Queens

One deck Extra game · download
Also known as
Quadrille

A game of pure luck. Goal: build the other 40 cards round the four Queens, who hold court in the middle and never move.

  • The four Fives and four Sixes are laid round the court to start eight foundations.
  • Each Five builds down in suit through the Ace to the King: 5 4 3 2 A K.
  • Each Six builds up in suit to the Jack: 6 7 8 9 10 J.
  • Tap the stock to turn one card at a time onto the waste. The top card of the waste is available.
  • When the stock is spent, tap it to turn the waste back over. You may do so twice (three passes in all).
  • Your score is the number of cards round the court, the Queens, Fives and Sixes among them, 52 for a win.
Read its design file 59 lines

No tableau at all: a stock, a waste and a radial layout, eight foundations turned along their radii round a cross of overlapping Queens, all placed by the deal. A Five's foundation wraps through the Ace to the King, and with the Queens out of play neither kind needs a cap. This is the file the download button gives you.
