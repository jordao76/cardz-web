Twenty holes, one waste to fill them

Carpet

One deck Extra game · download

Goal: move every card to the four foundations, building up by suit from Ace to King.

  • Twenty face-up cards form the carpet. Any carpet card may move to a foundation.
  • A hole in the carpet may be filled only with the top waste card; choosing when and where to fill holes is the heart of the game.
  • Draw the stock one card at a time to the waste. There is no recycle.
  • The foundations begin with the four Aces.
