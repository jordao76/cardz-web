Beat the fourth King

Clock

One deck

A game of pure luck. Goal: turn up all 52 cards before the fourth King appears.

  • Flip the top card of the central pile, then tuck it face-up under the hour pile matching its rank (Ace = 1 o'clock … Queen = 12, Kings = centre).
  • Continue from the top of the pile you just placed on.
