Unpick the weave

Crazy Quilt

Two decks

Goal: build all eight foundations to move every card home — four built up in suit from Ace to King, four built down in suit from King to Ace (two decks, so each suit fills one up-pile and one down-pile). The foundations start seeded with an Ace and a King of each suit.

  • The quilt is 64 cards woven in a lattice. A quilt card is available when either of its short ends is clear — i.e. one of the two neighbours it points at is gone (cards on the rim start free).
  • Play any available quilt card, or the waste top, onto a foundation.
  • You may also lay an available quilt card on the waste if it is one rank up or down (any suit, and Ace/King wrap) — this chains, and is the main way to dig the weave open. Waste cards then only advance to foundations.
  • Draw from the stock to the waste; recycle the waste when it runs out.
