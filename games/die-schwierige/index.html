Two decks, two rounds, the difficult one

Die Schwierige

Two decks Extra game · download

Goal: move all 104 cards to the eight foundations.

  • The foundations start with the eight Aces and build up in suit to King.
  • Tap the stock to deal a row of six onto the cascades. Only a cascade's top card can move, to a foundation or a sorting pile.
  • The four sorting piles build down in suit, one card at a time. An empty sorting pile takes any card.
  • In the first round, no card may be played onto a cascade.
  • When the stock is spent, tap it once for the second round: the cascades are picked up in order, unshuffled, with the bottom two sorting piles on top, so their cards come out last. Those two piles then close.
  • In the second round, an empty cascade takes the top card of a sorting pile.
  • Your score is the number of cards on the foundations, 104 for a win.
Read its design file 62 lines

Two rounds that play by different rules: accept rules that read how many redeals have run close two sorting piles and open the cascades once the stock is gathered back. The regather lists its piles so they deal out in the order they were picked up. This is the file the download button gives you. Make a game explains every part of it.
