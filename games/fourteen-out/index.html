Pairs that make fourteen, all in sight

Fourteen Out

One deck Extra game · download

Goal: remove all 52 cards in pairs that total 14.

  • All cards are dealt face up into twelve columns; the first four columns hold five cards and the other eight hold four.
  • Only the top card of each column is available.
  • Drag one available card to the discard, then add its partner: Ace + King, 2 + Queen, 3 + Jack, 4 + 10, 5 + 9, 6 + 8, or 7 + 7.
  • A completed pair turns face down and the next pair may be started.
Read its design file 22 lines
