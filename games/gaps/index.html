Two to King, one suit to a row

Gaps

One deck Extra game · download

Goal: order each row from Two to King in one suit.

  • The Aces are taken away, leaving four gaps.
  • Fill a gap with the card one higher, in the same suit, as the card on its left. A gap in the first column takes any Two.
  • A gap to the right of a King, or of another gap, stays empty.
  • Stuck? Tap the stock to redeal, twice at most. Each row keeps its run from a Two in the first column; the other cards are shuffled and dealt again, leaving one gap after each run.
