One rank away

Golf

One deck

Goal: clear all seven tableau columns onto the foundation.

  • Play the top card of any column onto the foundation if it is one rank up or down from the foundation's top card (no suit restriction).
  • There is no wrap: only a Queen plays on a King, and only a 2 plays on an Ace.
  • Stuck with no legal move and an empty stock? The game is lost.
  • Draw the stock onto the foundation to keep going (one pass only — no recycling).
