Grandfather's Clock

One deck Extra game · download

Goal: complete the clock. Each of its twelve foundations builds up in suit until its top card shows the hour it stands at: Jack for eleven, Queen for twelve, Ace for one.

  • The twelve starting cards are set out before the deal: 9♣ at twelve o'clock, then clockwise 10♥ J♠ Q♦ K♣ 2♥ 3♠ 4♦ 5♣ 6♥ 7♠ 8♦. Ranks wrap from King to Ace.
  • The other forty cards are dealt face up into eight columns of five, four each side of the clock.
  • Columns build down regardless of suit. Move one card at a time.
  • Any card may fill an empty column.
Read its design file 64 lines
