Red to the north, black to the south

Hemispheres

Two decks Extra game · download

Goal: move all 104 cards to the eight foundations.

  • The four red Aces build up in suit to King, the four black Kings down in suit to Ace.
  • Red cards belong to the north (the six piles in the top half of the circle), black cards to the south (the six in the bottom half). Only a pile's top card can move, one card at a time.
  • In the north, a red card builds down in suit on a red card; in the south, a black card builds up in suit on a black card. The waste's top card may build regardless of suit, onto a pile of its own colour.
  • A card in the wrong hemisphere is stranded: it can't move, and nothing builds on it. Exchange it by dropping it on a stranded card of the other colour across the equator: the two swap places.
  • Tap the stock to turn one card onto the waste. There is no redeal.
  • An empty pile is refilled at once from the stock, or from the waste once the stock is spent.
  • Once the stock is spent, stranded cards are free, and the four barriers lying crossways may finish their foundations.
  • Your score is the number of cards on the foundations, 104 for a win.
Read its design file 129 lines

An exchange made of a drop and a trigger: a stranded card lands only on a stranded card of the other colour, and a trigger on the stock sends the card beneath back where the arrival came from, then refills empty piles for the whole table.
