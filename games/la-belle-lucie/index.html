Eighteen fans, two redeals

La Belle Lucie

One deck

Goal: build all four foundations up from Ace to King, each in a single suit.

  • The board is eighteen fans. Build them down in the same suit (e.g. a ♥6 on a ♥7), one card at a time — only the exposed end card of a fan can move.
  • An emptied fan stays empty: once a fan is cleared, nothing can be placed back into it.
  • Stuck? Tap the stock to gather every fan, shuffle, and re-deal into threes. You may re-deal twice per game.
  • Merci: to free a card buried in a fan, turn on FISH (press F) and drag that card out — it will play wherever it legally lands. By tradition, once per game.

Eighteen fans that give up only their top card, one named rule shared by all of them, and two redeals that gather, shuffle and deal again. Written as a design file, this is the whole game. Make a game explains every part of it.
