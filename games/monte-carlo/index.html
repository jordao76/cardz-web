Pair off neighbours, close the gaps

Monte Carlo

One deck Extra game · download

Goal: pair off all 52 cards.

  • Twenty-five cards are dealt face up in a five by five grid.
  • Drop a card onto another of the same rank that touches it: beside it, above or below, or diagonally. The pair goes to the discard.
  • Tap the stock to consolidate: the remaining cards slide toward the top left, in reading order, to close the gaps, and the empty cells at the end are refilled from the stock.
  • You win when every card has been paired. You lose when no pair touches and there is nothing left to consolidate.
  • Your score is the number of cards removed, 52 for a win.
Read its design file 35 lines
