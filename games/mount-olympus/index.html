A pyramid of Aces and Twos, climbing by twos

Mount Olympus

Two decks Extra game · download

Goal: build both decks onto sixteen foundations. They build up by twos, in suit.

  • The eight Aces and eight Twos start the foundations, laid out as a pyramid. An Ace builds A 3 5 7 9 J K, a Two builds 2 4 6 8 10 Q, so a won game shows Kings and Queens alternating up each side.
  • The battery of nine piles builds down by twos, in suit: a 7 on a 9, a 10 on a Queen. Only the top card of each pile is available, and cards move one at a time.
  • A space in the battery is refilled at once from the waste, or from the stock while the waste is empty.
  • Turn the stock one card at a time to the waste; the top waste card is available.
  • You may redeal once: tap the empty stock to turn the waste back over.
  • Your score is the number of cards on the foundations, 104 for a win.
Read its design file 86 lines

Sixteen foundations placed one by one into an inverted V, each Ace and Two by name, building by twos without wrap, so an Ace pile stops at the King and a Two pile at the Queen with no cap. A battery building down by twos in suit, whose spaces take only their refill from the waste, then the stock, and a stock that turns the waste back once. A planner value counts each card turned from the stock, so Cardz draws rather than shuttle a card between two equal tops. This is the file the download button gives you.
