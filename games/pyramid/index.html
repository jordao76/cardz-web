Pairs add up to thirteen

Pyramid

One deck

The classic 28-card pyramid. Goal: clear the whole pyramid. A card can be lifted only once the two cards overlapping it from below are gone — the base row is free to start, the rest open up as you clear toward the apex. Remove cards in pairs that total 13 (A=1 … Q=12) by dragging them onto the discard; a King (13) goes alone. The stock's top turns face-up and is playable; set turned cards aside on the waste. Tip: the Scarab deck shows a small gold hint on each card — the rank it pairs with to reach 13.
