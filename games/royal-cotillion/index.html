Lords and ladies, dancing up by twos

Royal Cotillion

Two decks Extra game · download

Goal: build both decks onto eight foundations, thirteen cards each. They build up by twos, in suit, wrapping from King round to Ace.

  • An Ace and a Two of each suit start the foundations. An Ace builds A 3 5 7 9 J K 2 4 6 8 10 Q, a Two builds 2 4 6 8 10 Q A 3 5 7 9 J K, so a won game shows each suit's King and Queen side by side.
  • The ladies' side, on the left, holds four columns of three. Only their top cards are available, and a card taken from there is not replaced.
  • The lords' side, on the right, holds sixteen cards, all available. A space there is refilled at once from the waste, or from the stock while the waste is empty.
  • Cards play only to the foundations, never from one pile to another.
  • Turn the stock one card at a time to the waste; the top waste card is available. There is no redeal.
  • Your score is the number of cards on the foundations, 104 for a win.
Read its design file 57 lines

Foundations that build by twos round the corner from King to Ace, capped at thirteen cards by an expression, beside two reserves that give up cards differently: one only from its column tops, never refilled, the other all open and refilled from the waste, then the stock.
