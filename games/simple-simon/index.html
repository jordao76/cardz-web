Four perfect runs, every card in sight

Simple Simon

One deck Extra game · download

Goal: assemble four same-suit runs from King to Ace. Completed runs move to the foundations automatically.

  • All 52 cards start face up in ten columns. There is no stock or redeal.
  • Build columns down in any suit, without wrapping from Ace to King.
  • Move one card, or a same-suit descending sequence as a group. Mixed-suit groups cannot move together.
  • Any card or valid sequence may fill an empty column.
