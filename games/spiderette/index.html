Spider on a single deck

Spiderette

One deck Extra game · download

Goal: assemble four complete same-suit King-to-Ace runs and move them to the foundations.

  • Tableau columns build down in any suit — a card lands on any card one rank higher.
  • Cards move as a group only when they form a same-suit descending run; otherwise you move one card at a time.
  • Only face-up cards can be picked up; uncovering a face-down card flips it.
  • Any card may start an empty column.
  • When you're stuck, tap the stock to deal one card onto every column — allowed only when no column is empty. Its last three cards go to the first three columns.
  • A complete King-to-Ace run in a single suit leaves for a foundation on its own the moment you finish it.
