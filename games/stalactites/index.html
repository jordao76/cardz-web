Columns that only give, by ones or by twos

Stalactites

One deck Extra game · download

Goal: build every card onto four foundations, thirteen cards each. They build up regardless of suit, wrapping from King round to Ace, either by ones or by twos.

  • The first four cards dealt start the foundations, whatever their ranks.
  • Your first build chooses the step for all four foundations. On a 5, a 6 sets them building by ones (6 7 8 … K A … 4), a 7 by twos (7 9 J K 2 4 6 8 10 Q A 3).
  • The other forty-eight cards are dealt face up into eight columns of six.
  • Nothing is built on the columns. Their top cards go to a foundation or to one of the two cells, and an emptied column stays empty.
  • Each cell holds one card, which may go only to a foundation.
  • Your score is the number of cards on the foundations, 52 for a win.
Read its design file 34 lines

A choice the player makes by playing: the foundations' accept rule reads the step off the first foundation built on, so the first build sets every foundation going by ones or by twos, and undoing it undoes the choice. A depot property finds that step, and columns accept nothing, so they are only a source. This is the file the download button gives you. Make a game explains every part of it.
