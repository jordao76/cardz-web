Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Cardz and Cardz Lite are single-player card games that run entirely on your device. This policy covers both.

What we collect

Nothing, unless you choose to send it. Cardz does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information on its own. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or telemetry. The only thing that can reach us is a crash report you decide to email, described below.

Data stored on your device

Cardz saves the following locally so your games and preferences persist between sessions. This data never leaves your computer unless you send it:

  • Your game in progress, so it resumes where you left it.
  • Your statistics and scores.
  • Your settings, such as the chosen deck.
  • In Cardz, the games you keep, write or import, and the decks you import.
  • A log of errors and of the app's last few steps, so that a crash can be explained.

This data lives in the app's local storage and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Cardz Lite also keeps a copy of your statistics and scores in a folder on your PC that it shares with Cardz, so that Cardz can pick them up if you install it. The copy never leaves your computer, and Windows removes it once neither app is installed.

Network access

Cardz works fully offline and makes no network connections in normal use. It does not require the internet to play.

Crash reports

If the app closes unexpectedly, it says so the next time you open it and offers to report it. Nothing is sent unless you choose to: Email report opens a message to cardz@roderic.dev in your own email app, and Copy details puts the same report on your clipboard. You can read and change the message before sending it, or not send it at all.

A report holds technical details only: the app's version, the versions of Windows and .NET, when the session started, the error that closed it, the app's last few steps (such as which screen was open), and where its log files are on your PC. That location includes your Windows user name.

If you email a report, we receive it with your email address, and use them only to look into the problem and to reply. They are not shared with anyone.

Separately, Windows may offer to send a diagnostic report to the developer through the Microsoft Store's standard health reporting. These reports contain technical crash information only (such as the location in the code where the error occurred) and no personal data. They are governed by Microsoft's privacy statement.

Children's privacy

Cardz does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new “last updated” date.
