Cards as physical things

Small verbs.
Endless moves.

Drag to move, double-tap to flip, and drop near a depot to snap. That is all you need. When you want more control, arm a mode for the next gesture—or hold it for repeated use.

The modes answer different physical questions: what comes with the card, how the cards should land, whether they travel above or below, and what a double-tap should do. Many combine naturally.

Table modes & tools

A

All

Pick up every card in the connected pile. On a double-tap, flip the whole group together.

S

Spread

Open the cards into a freehand fan while you drag, choosing its direction and shape yourself.

C

Corral

Gather a scattered cluster into a pile, column, row, or fan—right-click the chip to pick the shape.

D

Order

Reposition a card within its own fan while neighbouring cards slide aside to make room.

F

Fish

Pluck one card from anywhere in a pile without disturbing the cards above it.

G

Grab

Take the top N cards as a packet. Cycle common counts or choose any number from two to nine.

R

Rotate

Turn a card as you drag. Combine it with All, Spread, Fish, or Grab for group rotations.

V

Dive

Send the dragged cards behind the table instead of in front, tucking them under another pile.

T

Trawl

Sweep across the table and collect loose cards into the group already under your pointer.

L

Shuffle

Make the next tap shuffle the chosen pile in place—or, on its second setting, sort it into order instead of flipping it.

O

Orient

Set every card in scope face-up or face-down—perfect for tidying a mixed pile.

P

Depot

Stamp a rule-free landing spot—pile, column, row, or fan—onto empty felt, then drop cards to keep them tidy. Right-click to choose its shape.

Keep

Save the layout you've arranged for another game. Update a layout you've already kept, or save a new version.

About keeping layouts →

The twelve table modes are one-shot by default, can be held for repeated gestures, and compose into moves such as Fish + Grab, Spread + Grab, All + Rotate, and Grab + Order. Keep saves your layout directly, without changing the next gesture.

A table worth coming back to

Arrange it.
Keep it.

Set up a table for the way you like to play, then keep the layout so you can use it again.

  1. Make room for your game

    Use Depot to place piles, columns, rows or fans. Move these landing spots where you want them.

  2. Give it a name

    Choose Keep to save your layout. When you rearrange a layout you've kept, you can update it or save a new version.

  3. Come back and play

    Find it in Your games. Start a new game with a full stock of cards and your landing spots ready. Rename, duplicate, export or delete your layouts there, too.

Keep saves your table layout and deck setup. The cards you've dealt and their current positions aren't part of the saved layout, and the table stays rule-free—you decide how to play.
